VisionX-AI / FAQ

Factory questions, answered clearly.

Why should we choose VisionX-AI?

  • 90%-95% AI detection accuracy
  • Real-time camera-based inspection
  • Customizable solutions for factory requirements
  • Strong AI, software, and research team
  • Industry-focused garment manufacturing expertise
  • Practical, factory-driven problem-solving

How does the AI system work?

The system captures images through cameras in real time and uses AI to detect defects, quality issues, color variations, and production errors automatically.

What defects can VisionX-AI detect?

It can detect stitching defects, raw edges, seam issues, bartack defects, pocket defects, waistband defects, thread-related defects, color variations, and other quality problems. It can also be trained to detect custom factory-specific defects.

What is the accuracy of the system?

The AI system typically achieves around 90% accuracy depending on product type, defect category, camera setup, and factory conditions.

Can the system be customized for our factory?

Yes. The solution can be customized based on your production process, defect types, factory layout, reporting requirements, and quality control workflow.

Where can the system be used?

  • Quality inspection stations
  • Sewing lines
  • Fabric defect detection stations
  • Washing and color detection areas
  • Production monitoring stations
  • Final quality control sections

Does the system provide reports and analytics?

Yes. VisionX-AI provides real-time quality reports, defect tracking, trend analysis, and management dashboards. By improving consistency and product quality, factories can reduce claims, strengthen buyer confidence, improve compliance, and increase buyer satisfaction.

Are you currently working with factories?

Yes. Nextway Global is currently working on projects with Tarasima and CKDL Factory to improve quality control and operational efficiency through AI technology.
